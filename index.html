About

I’m a finance graduate from the University of Ghana Business School with an MPhil in Finance and a background in mathematics. My work sits where quantitative risk meets practical fintech.

On the research side, I model how shocks travel between commodities, stocks and currencies in African markets, using quantile and tail-dependence methods in R and Python. On the product side, I design tools for problems I see every day in Ghana: mobile money fraud, and traders who cannot get credit because they have no records.

Before academia, I spent several years in credit operations at Zenith Bank Ghana, Letshego and SIC Life, so I know how lending decisions are made on the ground.
